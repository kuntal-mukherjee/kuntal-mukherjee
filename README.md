
# Hi, I'm Dr. Kuntal Mukherjee 👋

### PhD in Chemistry | Magnetic Resonance Methodology | Theoretical and Computational Spin Dynamics

I recently completed my PhD in Chemistry at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Mohali, India, under the supervision of Prof. Ramesh Ramachandran.

My research focuses on the theoretical and computational description of spin dynamics in solid-state NMR, particularly polarization transfer, coherence evolution, and dipolar recoupling under Magic Angle Spinning (MAS) conditions.

I am interested in applying mathematical modelling, numerical simulations, and magnetic resonance methodology to investigate complex spin systems and contribute to the development of quantitative magnetic resonance techniques.

---

## 🔬 Research Interests

- Magnetic resonance methodology and spin dynamics
- Solid-state NMR under Magic Angle Spinning
- Dipolar recoupling and polarization transfer
- Coherence evolution in multi-spin systems
- Average and effective Hamiltonian theory
- Floquet theory and perturbative approaches
- Computational modelling of magnetic resonance experiments

## 📂 Research Projects

### Computational Modelling of Dipolar Recoupling

Analytical and numerical investigation of polarization transfer and coherence evolution in solid-state NMR experiments under MAS conditions.

### Spin Dynamics in Multi-Spin Systems

Study of active and passive spin contributions, including proton driven depolarization effects, in dipolar recoupling experiments.

### Numerical Simulation of NMR Experiments

Simulation and analysis of spin dynamics using NMR simulation tools and custom computational workflows.
